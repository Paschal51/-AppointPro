NEED HELP?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about appointments and the booking process.

How do I book an appointment?

Choose a service, complete the appointment form, select your preferred date and time, and submit your request. Wait for confirmation from the provider.

Is my appointment confirmed immediately?

Not necessarily. On this website, submitting the form creates a request. The service provider must confirm the appointment before it is considered booked.

Can I change my appointment?

Contact the service provider to request a new date or time. Changes depend on availability and the provider's scheduling policy.

What happens if I cannot attend?

Contact the service provider as early as possible to ask about cancellation or rescheduling options.

Can I choose a specific professional?

Yes. If the service offers professional selection, you can choose a preferred professional in the booking form. Availability must be checked separately.

Is online booking available at all times?

You can design the website to accept requests at any time. Actual appointment availability depends on the business schedule and booking system.
